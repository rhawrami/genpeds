# Harmonization guide

This guide describes **what `genpeds` actually downloads and transforms**, with the source-year boundaries that matter for analysis. The companion [variable codebook](variables.csv) documents every public `get_available_vars()` entry; [changes.csv](changes.csv) lists the specific change and its NCES dictionary; [codes.csv](codes.csv) records selected category codes. The package's `src/genpeds/cleaners.py`, `core.py`, and `cfg.json` are the implementation of record.

IPEDS publishes **institution-level aggregates**, not person-level records. A question without a gender split can still be useful and is included where supported. Unless specified, the package does not impute missing values or construct a long-run crosswalk between changed survey concepts.

## Year and join semantics

The class constructor accepts a single year, inclusive `(start, end)` tuple, or list of years. `scrape()` caches downloaded source files in a subject-named directory in the working directory; `clean()` reads the cache; `run()` does both. Most `run()` methods can `merge_with_char=True` to join `Characteristics` on `id` (NCES `UNITID`) **and the API's `year`**. `rm_disk=True` removes the downloaded directories used by that run. No merge changes what the source file's year measures.

| Output | Meaning of `year` | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Characteristics`, `Admissions`, `Enrollment`, `DistanceEnrollment` | Header / admissions / fall snapshot year | `Characteristics(2025)` describes the 2025–26 IC collection; both fall-enrollment APIs currently end in 2024. |
| `TwelveMonthEnrollment`, `InstructionalActivity` | End of July–June enrollment/activity period | `year=2025`, `period_start_year=2024`: July 2024–June 2025. |
| `Retention` | Follow-up fall; **not** the entering cohort year | `year=2024`, `cohort_year=2023`. |
| `Tuition` | **Start** of published academic/program price year | `year=2024` prices for 2024–25. |
| `StudentAid`, `VeteransAid` | **End** of aid/benefit year | `year=2024`, `aid_year_start=2023`: aid during 2023–24; `SFA2324`. |
| `HumanResources` | Fall workforce snapshot or **start** of salary academic year | `year=2024` is fall/November 2024 staff, 2024–25 salary, or Nov 2023–Oct 2024 hires. |
| `Finance` | **End** of the reported fiscal year | `year=2024` is fiscal 2024, reported in the 2024–25 Spring collection (`F2324_F1A/F2/F3`). |
| `AcademicLibraries` | **End** of the reported fiscal year | `year=2024` is fiscal 2024 (`AL2024`), collected in 2024–25, the final AL survey. |
| `Completion`, `Completers` | **End** of July–June award period | `C2025_A/B/C` covers July 2024–June 2025; no six-year cohort is implied. |
| `Graduation` | Graduation-rate reporting/status year for an **older entering cohort** | Associate/bachelor 150%-of-normal-time cohorts started about three/six years earlier. |
| `Graduation200` | GR200 status year for a **different older cohort** | `year=2024`: bachelor's `cohort_year=2016`; less-than-four-year `cohort_year=2020`. |
| `OutcomeMeasures` | Eight-year follow-up/status year for July–June entrants | `year=2024` follows **2016–17** entrants to August 2024; four/six-year checkpoints are in 2020/2022. |
| `Cip` | Year of the Completions **data dictionary** | Describes that year's code; it does not date an entering cohort. |

**A shared `year` does not guarantee the same time window**: `Tuition(2024)` covers 2024–25 prices while `StudentAid(2024)` covers 2023–24 aid. A 2025 Characteristics merge onto `TwelveMonthEnrollment(2025)` attaches a later fall institutional snapshot to the preceding July–June period. Align the period explicitly before comparing or joining prices, aid, enrollment, and awards.

The directory of [configured sources](source_files.csv) preserves the exact ZIP stems (including historical renames, lower-case names, and finance-style academic-year names where applicable). The package uses `https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/` for its configured years through 2022 and `https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/` afterward. `Cip` downloads the **Completions dictionary** instead of the awards CSV. Dictionary link examples: [C2000_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2000_A_Dict.zip), [C2001_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2001_A_Dict.zip), [C2025_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/C2025_A_Dict.zip).

| API class | Current coverage | Output grain / selector | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Characteristics` | 1984–2025 | Institution-year; **1986 exception** below | Year-dependent IC/FA/HD headers. |
| `Admissions` | 2001–2024 | Institution-year; first-time applicant/admit/enroll measures | IC through 2013, ADM from 2014. |
| `Enrollment` | 1984–2024 | Institution-fall year × `studentlevel` (`undergrad` or `grad`) | EF-A fall level/attendance lines. |
| `DistanceEnrollment` | 2012–2024 | Institution-fall year × EFDELEV level; `'all'` includes overlapping UG and institution totals | EF-A_DIST distance supplement. |
| `TwelveMonthEnrollment` | Periods ending 2002–2025 | Institution-period × `student_level` (`undergrad`, `grad`, `total`, early `first_professional`); `'all'` includes overlapping total | EFFY. |
| `InstructionalActivity` | Periods ending 2002–2025 | One institution × July–June period; hours and separately estimated/reported FTE in columns | EFIA. |
| `Retention` | Follow-up falls 2003–2024 | Institution-follow-up fall; FT/PT rates and later cohort counts as separate columns | EF-D. |
| `Tuition` | Price years starting 2000–2024 | Institution-price year × `reporter` (`academic`, `program`) | IC `_AY/_PY` through 2023; COST1 in 2024. |
| `StudentAid` | Aid years ending 2002–2024 | Institution-aid year × `reporter`; FTFT and all-UG measures in **different columns** | SFA; COST2 for 2024 net price. |
| `VeteransAid` | Aid years ending 2014–2024 | Institution-aid year; UG/graduate and program counts in separate columns | SFAV. |
| `HumanResources` | 2012–2024 | One selected HR dataset with its own institution-year × staff/occupation/rank/faculty-status row grain | EAP, S*_OC/IS/SIS/NH, SAL*_IS/NIS. |
| `Finance` | Fiscal years ending 2004–2024 | Institution × fiscal year × F1A/F2/F3 form, one row per source-file institution | Three annual Finance accounting forms. |
| `AcademicLibraries` | Fiscal years ending 2014–2024 | Institution × fiscal year; counts, FTE, expenses and reporting screens in distinct columns | Annual AL files, retired after 2024–25. |
| `Completion` | Award years ending 1984–2025 | Institution-year × CIP × selected `deglevel`/`major_type`; **awards by field**, not unique people | C-A (older differently named extracts). |
| `Completers` | Award years ending 2012–2025 | B: institution-year distinct people across all awards; C: institution-year-award level distinct people with age | C-B or C-C (modern files only). |
| `Graduation` | 2000–2024 | Institution-status year × selected associate/bachelor cohort | GR, selected 150% rows. |
| `Graduation200` | 2008–2024 | Institution-status year × applicable bachelor or less-than-four-year cohort | GR200_YY (initial 2008 supplemental wave). |
| `OutcomeMeasures` | 2015–2024 | Institution-status year × entry cohort; 2017+ includes overlapping overall and Pell/non-Pell groups | OM; initial 2015–16 structure differs. |
| `Cip` | 1984–2025 | CIP code × year description | Completions data dictionary. |

`Characteristics(1986)` includes **different institutions with the same placeholder `UNITID=247719`**. It returns the source rows, but subject classes reject `merge_with_char=True` when the selected header has duplicate `(id, year)` keys; otherwise a join could multiply observations. No artificial ID crosswalk is constructed. A UNITID may also change over decades as institutions split or combine.

## Subject-by-subject harmonization

### Characteristics

The cleaner trims identifiers, title-cases name/address/city, expands `STABBR` to a state name, and selects URL from 1999 and coordinates from 2009. It preserves raw `CONTROL`, `ICLEVEL`, `SECTOR`, `HBCU`, `TRIBAL`, `DEGGRANT`, `LOCALE`, and `C21BASIC` as `*_code`, alongside mapped labels/nullable booleans. Raw codes such as `-3` remain visible even if no label is assigned.

- **1984–85** `CONTROL=2` is simply *private*, not the **1986+** `CONTROL=2` *private nonprofit*; `CONTROL=0` also has an early combined-public/private meaning. `ICLEVEL=7` in 1984–85 means unclassified, **not** less-than-two-year (later code 3). See [IC1984 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC1984_Dict.zip) and [IC1986_A dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC1986_A_Dict.zip).
- HBCU starts **1992**, tribal **1993**. Early blank/no values (1992–94) are handled differently from **1995–97** `-1` missing/nonresponse; later 1/2 mean yes/no. The API keeps nullable booleans rather than turning an unknown into `False` ([IC1993_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC1993_A_Dict.zip), [IC98hdac](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC98hdac_Dict.zip)).
- `LOCALE` starts **1995**, and changes from 1–9 legacy urbanization codes to **11–43 urban-centric** codes in **2005**. `locale_scheme` identifies the scheme. [codes.csv](codes.csv) records their labels; the API does **not** claim a one-to-one crosswalk. `carnegie_2021_basic_code` starts **2021** and is intentionally named for its vintage.

### Admissions

Year-dependent IC/ADM raw columns are renamed; the cleaner retains men/women applicant, admit and enrollee counts, including available full-time/part-time enrollees. In **2001** FT and PT male/female enrollment are added before yields are computed, and absent FT/PT/source application/admission/enrollment totals are reconstructed from men + women. From **2002**, when reported totals exist, those—not a binary reconstruction—are used. Acceptance = admitted/applied × 100; yield = enrolled/admitted × 100 (overall and by men/women), with zero denominators missing. Men’s shares divide by the source or constructed total. Recent sources contain **another/unknown-gender counts from 2022**, which the cleaner retains as supplemental fields: they need not be disjoint from the binary counts used in survey reporting. Source `SATPCT`/`ACTPCT` describes **first-time degree/certificate-seeking score submitters**, not the share of all admitted applicants. `SATNUM`/`ACTNUM` are the corresponding submitter **counts** from 2001; score medians and raw additional-gender counts appear from **2022**. Test-score definitions and applicability vary by year; consult each dictionary ([IC2001](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC2001_Dict.zip), [ADM2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/ADM2024_Dict.zip)).

`ADMCON1`–`ADMCON8` considerations exist from **2001**, `ADMCON9` from **2005**, and work experience / essay / legacy (`ADMCON10`–`12`) from **2022**. The API preserves the source `consider_*_code` (including nonresponse/not-applicable codes) and maps recognized values to year-specific `consider_*` labels. Through 2015 codes 1/2/3 meant required/recommended/neither required nor recommended; **2016–21** adds code 5 considered-but-not-required; **2022+** uses 1 required-to-be-considered, 5 considered-if-submitted (test-optional for scores) and 3 not considered (test-blind for scores). A raw code cannot be read as an invariant ordinal policy measure; see [code mapping](codes.csv) and [ADM2016](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/ADM2016_Dict.zip) / [ADM2022](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/ADM2022_Dict.zip).

### Fall Enrollment

`Enrollment` selects specific EF-A `LINE` rows for the requested level, then sums FT/PT counts by institution and calculates sex/race shares. UG uses lines **1/15 in 1984–85** and **8/22 from 1986**. Graduate lines are year-dependent: **1984–85** `11,25,10,24`; **1986 and 1990–98** `14,28,9,10,23,24`; **1987–89** `14,28`; **1999** `32,52,16`; **2000–08** `11,25,9,23`; **2009–24** `11,25`. Some older graduate rules include separately counted first-professional students. This is a fall snapshot, not an unduplicated annual count. Older `EFRACE##` fields and newer `EFTOTL*` / named race fields are selected according to which occur in a file. Only White, Black, Hispanic, Asian and men/women totals are exposed; other NCES race categories are not included in this API. Shares use the sum of **men + women** as denominator; do not interpret these as every category in expanded gender reporting. See the historical [EF1984](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EF1984_Dict.zip) and modern [EF2024A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/EF2024A_Dict.zip) dictionaries.

### DistanceEnrollment

Separate `EF2012A_DIST`–`EF2024A_DIST` files count **fall students enrolled for credit**, by whether they take exclusively distance education courses (`EFDEEXC`), some but not exclusively distance courses (`EFDESOM`), or none (`EFDENON`). An IPEDS distance education *course* delivers its instructional content exclusively at a distance: on-campus orientation, testing or academic support visits do not by themselves change that classification. These are counts of **students, not courses**. The cleaner keeps NCES values, computes category / `EFDETOT` × 100 shares (zero denominator → missing), and preserves matching `X...` reporting flags. [EF2012A_DIST](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EF2012A_DIST_Dict.zip), [EF2024A_DIST](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/EF2024A_DIST_Dict.zip).

`EFDELEV=1` is **all students**, `2` all undergraduates, `3` degree/certificate-seeking undergraduates, `11` non-degree undergraduates, `12` graduate students. `student_level='all'` returns all these **overlapping rows**: do not sum level 1 with levels 2/12 or level 2 with its subgroups 3/11. The five `EFDEEX1`–`EFDEEX5` geography fields subdivide **exclusively** distance students only (same state; another U.S. state; U.S. state unknown; outside the U.S.; location unknown). They are not geographic breakdowns of the some-distance group. This source has a different grain from `Enrollment` (which first aggregates FT/PT by level) and `TwelveMonthEnrollment` (a July–June unduplicated count), so the package keeps a separate class.

### TwelveMonthEnrollment

`year` is the ending year of the July–June period. EFFY2002–07 uses `FYRACE15/16/24` for men/women/grand total; from **2008**, `EFYTOTLM/LW/LT` and separate modern Asian/Pacific Islander fields appear. The older `asian_pacific` combined category is **not** added to or equated with the later `asian` / `pacific_islander` counts. Other race fields also inherit NCES's changing classifications. `_status` fields preserve raw X flags where present.

Through **2019**, `LSTUDY` selects a level row (`1` UG, `3` graduate, `999` generated total; `2` first-professional through **2010**). From **2020**, `EFFYALEV` codes `2` UG, `12` graduate, `1` institution total are selected; many *other* EFFYALEV rows are nested degree-seeking/attendance/transfer subsets and must not be summed. In `student_level='all'` the total **overlaps** the other rows. The early graduate count excludes separately reported first-professional students; later graduate counts include them, so a direct graduate trend crosses a definition break. See [EFFY2002](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EFFY2002_Dict.zip), [EFFY2011](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EFFY2011_Dict.zip) and [EFFY2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/EFFY2025_Dict.zip). Earlier combined 12-month data also occur in EF-D, but this class intentionally starts at the separately configured **2002 EFFY** series.

### InstructionalActivity

The separate EFIA2002–EFIA2025 file has **one institution-year row**, not EFFY's overlapping student-level rows. The `year`/`period_start_year` identify the same July–June period as `TwelveMonthEnrollment`. `CDACTUA` is UG credit-hour activity, `CNACTUA` UG occupational-program contact/clock-hour activity, and `CDACTGA` graduate credit-hour activity. These are **instructional hour totals**, not unique students or quantities that can simply be added across different hour units. `ACTTYPE` from **2004** says whether reporting uses contact/clock (`1`), credit (`2`), both (`3`), or is not applicable (`-2`). The dictionary's wording for `CNACTUA` changes from **contact** through EFIA2018 to **clock** in EFIA2019 onward. The API preserves `hour_term` and a year-aware `activity_type` label; it does not equate contact/clock hours with credit hours. [EFIA2002](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EFIA2002_Dict.zip), [EFIA2018](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EFIA2018_Dict.zip), [EFIA2019](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EFIA2019_Dict.zip), [EFIA2025](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/EFIA2025_Dict.zip).

**Estimated** UG/graduate FTE (`EFTEUG/EFTEGD`) and **reported** UG/graduate FTE (`FTEUG/FTEGD`) begin in **2004**; all four remain missing in 2002–03. NCES estimates FTE from credit and contact/clock hours using formulas that depend on calendar system. The reported FTE is not always an independent institutional measure: the dictionaries say an institution could supply its own estimate if the NCES estimate was unreasonable, **and if none was supplied NCES set reported FTE to estimated FTE**. The separately reported professional-practice doctoral FTE `FTEDPP` appears in **2012** and is not blindly summed into graduate FTE. Original `X...` status flags remain paired with each of the eight source measures. To compare 12-month UG headcount to UG FTE, select an EFFY `student_level='undergrad'` row and join on `(id, year)`, then interpret people and full-time equivalents as **different measures**. A `merge_with_char=True` join additionally attaches the header snapshot for the ending year, not the same July–June measurement.

### Retention

`year` is the follow-up fall, `cohort_year` is the prior fall. EF2003D–EF2006D report `RET_PCF/RET_PCP` FT/PT **published rates** but not their underlying cohort counts. **2007** adds FT/PT cohorts, exclusions, adjusted cohorts and retained students; **2016** adds study-abroad inclusions to the denominator. Before introduction those fields remain missing, not zero. Raw X flags are exposed as `*_status`; a 2004 `RET_PCP ` trailing-space header is normalized. The API keeps NCES's published percentages instead of back-computing rounded rates. [`EF2003D`](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EF2003D_Dict.zip), [`EF2007D`](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EF2007D_Dict.zip), [`EF2016D`](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/EF2016D_Dict.zip).

### Tuition

`Tuition` returns `academic`, `program`, or `both` reporting types. AY has in-district/in-state/out-of-state full-time undergraduate tuition, required fees and *separately published* FTFT tuition-plus-fees; these are not always arithmetic sums of the separate fields. PY has largest-program tuition-plus-fees (`CHG1PY3`) and a separate `CIPTUIT1` measure for programs **without** FTFT undergraduates from **2006**. Published amounts are nominal dollars, not aid-adjusted net price. Both IC*_AY/PY file families start in **2000**. The **2001** files unusually include universe-wide nonreporter rows, which the cleaner filters using empty AY prices or PY `CIPCODE1=-2`. `COST1_2024` combines both and uses that marker to distinguish reporters. `CHG*AY3` / `CHG1PY3` refer to the **current** listed price year; other fields in the same file repeat prior-year prices. [IC2001 price dictionaries](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC2001_AY_Dict.zip), [IC2006_PY](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/IC2006_PY_Dict.zip), [COST1_2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/COST1_2024_Dict.zip).

### StudentAid and VeteransAid

`StudentAid` separates FTFT aid (`ftft_*`) from aid to *all* undergraduates (`ug_*`). Early denominator fields are `SCFA1N/SCFA2` (academic-year reporters) or `SCFY1N/SCFY2` (program-year reporters); later shared `SCUGFFN/SCUGRAD` are used when populated. The output's `reporter` identifies the relevant cohort. Long-running FTFT federal/state/institutional grants and student loans are present from **aid year ending 2002**. Separate FTFT Pell, combined grant and federal-loan recipient categories begin **2008**; all-UG recipient counts and dollar totals begin **2009**. FTFT **student loans** can include nonfederal loans and are not interchangeable with the federal-loan subset. Grant recipient categories can overlap: adding categories does not yield unique people. `*_avg` amounts are among recipients, not all enrollees. `ftft_any_aid_pct` is NCES's reported percentage, not a recalculation.

Average `ftft_net_price` measures **cost of attendance after qualifying grants for in-state/in-district FTFT grant recipients**, not tuition or a cash award. Its 2009–23 values are from `SFA*_Dict`-documented `NPIST2`; for the **2023–24 aid year** the field moved from SFA to **`COST2_2024`**, which is left-joined on institution so schools absent from the Cost supplement remain in SFA with missing net price. `year=2024` means *aid 2023–24*. NCES wording changes from received to awarded across years and the 2024 Cost calculation changes housing terminology; do not read these as a single student-level financial panel. [SFA0102](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/SFA0102_Dict.zip), [SFA0809](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/SFA0809_Dict.zip), [SFA2324](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/SFA2324_Dict.zip), [COST2_2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/COST2_2024_Dict.zip).

`VeteransAid` is **separate**: SFAV from aid year ending **2014** reports undergraduate and graduate Post-9/11 GI Bill/DoD Tuition Assistance counts, totals and per-recipient averages. SFAV includes **graduate-only institutions absent from SFA**; folding it into an SFA left join would lose them. Benefits represent amounts **known to the institution**, not all benefits an individual necessarily received. [SFAV1314](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/SFAV1314_Dict.zip), [SFAV2324](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/SFAV2324_Dict.zip).

### Finance

`Finance(years).run(form='f1a'|'f2'|'f3'|'all')` reads three **distinct** institutional statements. The year is the **fiscal ending year**: F0304 is FY2004 and F2324 is FY2024; it is not the fall/salary year of HR or the current price year of Tuition. `form='all'` keeps accounting forms as separate rows; [codes.csv](codes.csv) records the form labels. F1A is **public GASB**, F2 is **private nonprofit plus a few public FASB institutions**, and F3 is **private for-profit FASB**. In the verified FY2024 source files their UNITIDs are disjoint, but the grain explicitly retains the form; do not assume that a school's `Characteristics.control` always determines its Finance form. No student or sex breakdown is reported. `merge_with_char=True` joins the header for the fiscal-ending calendar year, a different reporting window. All dollar amounts are nominal and retain their source X reporting/imputation flags (not applicable and blank remain missing, not zero). A single-form offline `clean(form='f2', finance_dir=...)` accepts an alternate cache directory. The three default cache directories are `financedata/`, `finance_f2data/`, and `finance_f3data/`.

The API covers **every year 2004–2024** for all three form filenames (63 data ZIPs checked with HTTP HEAD on 2026-09-26). FY2024 Spring data was **provisional** at that time, while FY2023 was final; NCES can update a ZIP. The earlier `F1984`, `F9697_F1/F2`, and FY2002 `F0102_F1/F1A/F2/F3` files exist, but their common-form, FASB phase-in and GASB transition rules differ, so they are **not appended** to this curated series. The live Complete Data Files file list could not be enumerated; lack of a guessed early ZIP stem does not establish a missing fiscal year. NCES [FASB/GASB comparison](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/report-your-data/data-tip-sheet-distinguishing-finance-standards-fasb-gasb) describes these breaks; the [Delta Cost Project finance harmonization](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data/delta-cost-project-finance-data) covers earlier fiscal years through 2011–12 but is a separate derived series, not this API.

For 2004–09 the API records `accounting_regime='prealigned_2004_2009'`; 2010 onward uses the aligned form, and F3 records `aligned_f3_revised_2014_onward` from **fiscal 2014**, reported in the 2014–15 collection. **Even within a form** this is not a fixed-purchasing-power or fully comparable trend: Pell pass-through and tuition allowances affect revenues; GASB operating/nonoperating classifications and FASB investment return differ; public pension/OPEB liabilities and expenses also change. `net_tuition_fees` uses source F1B01/F2D01/F3D01 (after allowances); `tuition_discounts` is the corresponding source allowance, not a second receipt. F1A GASB total **revenues and other additions** (F1B25) includes capital/endowment additions and is intentionally separate from F2/F3 **revenues and investment return** (F2D16/F3D09). Year-end GASB `net_position` (F1A18, called *total net assets* in older dictionaries), nonprofit FASB `net_assets` and for-profit FASB `equity` are kept in three distinct columns. F1A `operating_revenues`/`nonoperating_revenues` have no automatic FASB equivalents. `state_appropriations` does not include all state grants/contracts. Original dictionary links: [F0304_F1A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F0304_F1A_Dict.zip), [F0304_F2](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F0304_F2_Dict.zip), [F0304_F3](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F0304_F3_Dict.zip), [F2324_F1A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/F2324_F1A_Dict.zip), [F2324_F2](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/F2324_F2_Dict.zip), [F2324_F3](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/F2324_F3_Dict.zip).

The **for-profit F3 functional instruction/expense table** starts in FY2014 (`F3E011`, `F3E071`); earlier values are missing even though FY2004–13 have financial-statement `F3B02` total expenses. The API **does not substitute** F3 functional `functional_expenses` for `total_expenses`: in the FY2024 release F3B02 is applicable for only **484 of 2,046** F3 rows, while F3E071 is populated for 2,046; among those reporting both, **21 differ**. For F1A/F2 `total_expenses` is the source functional/expenses-and-deductions total (F1C191/F2E131); the form explains different concepts. FY2020 adds detailed **Pell discounts** (F1E12/F2C12/F3C12); FY2019 is missing these fields, not reporting zero. A 2024 `total_assets` with flag `A` likewise indicates not applicable rather than zero for some F3 reporters. See [F1314_F3](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F1314_F3_Dict.zip), [F1819_F1A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F1819_F1A_Dict.zip), [F1920_F1A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/F1920_F1A_Dict.zip), and the other form-specific dictionaries in [source_files.csv](source_files.csv).

### Academic Libraries

`AcademicLibraries` covers **AL2014–AL2024**, the annual IPEDS Academic Libraries component reintegrated in the **2014–15 collection**. It ended after **2024–25** ([NCES 2025–26 changes](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/report-your-data/archived-changes/2025-26)). All eleven year-specific data and dictionary ZIP URLs returned HTTP 200 on 2026-09-26; the **FY2024 release was provisional** when checked. The earlier biennial Academic Libraries Survey (ALS, separate public-use files through 2012) has a different universe and definitions, and is **not** appended to this API. `year` denotes the fiscal year described by the AL filename. `merge_with_char=True` joins the same-year institutional header by `UNITID`, not an identical survey snapshot. The [NCES AL/ALS history](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/report-your-data/resource-center-academic-libraries) explains the redesign.

One `(id, year)` row preserves **NCES amounts rather than computing new totals**. Physical/digital books (`LPBOOKS/LEBOOKS`), media (`LPMEDIA/LEMEDIA`), databases (`LEDATAB`), serials (`LPSERIA/LESERIA`), physical/electronic collections (`LPCLLCT/LECLLCT`) and circulations (`LPCRCLT/LECRCLT`) represent *different* counts. Source combined `LTCLLCT/LTCRCLT` totals begin in **2015**; separate serial counts begin in **2016**. The dictionary's 2016 headings can suggest serials are already part of `LPCLLCT/LECLLCT`, but its long descriptions explicitly say the computed collection totals **exclude serials through FY2018** and **include them from FY2019**. `collection_total_definition` exposes that break; don't splice raw aggregate totals as an invariant holdings measure. Circulation and interlibrary loans received/provided measure different activities, not owned stock. Counts of licensed digital access and vendor-provided usage can vary with source reporting. [AL2014](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/AL2014_Dict.zip), [AL2015](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/AL2015_Dict.zip), [AL2016](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/AL2016_Dict.zip), [AL2019](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/AL2019_Dict.zip), [AL2024](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/AL2024_Dict.zip).

Only libraries spending **at least $100,000** supply detailed expenditure items. `LEXP100K` explicitly screens `1` yes / `2` no from **2019**; before then `expenditure_threshold_code`/label remain missing, even though a nonreporting library may have `LFRNGBYN=-2`. `total_expenditures` is NCES `LEXPTOT` (salary/wages, fringe, materials/services and operations/maintenance); `expenditures_excluding_fringe` is the separate **NCES-calculated** `LSWMSOM`, created to aid older-survey comparisons and **does not have an X flag**. Missing detailed amounts for below-threshold libraries are not zeros. Individual spending components and their included totals are not additive to each other. `LFRNGBYN=-2` means not applicable, not a finding of zero fringe. From **2020**, `LSTOTAL/LSLIBRN/LSOPROF/LSOPAID/LSSTAST` provide full-time-equivalent library staffing, including student assistants; they are *not HR employee headcounts* and stay missing in 2014–19. Categorical `LCOLELYN`, `LILLDYN` (from 2016), `LILSYN` (from 2020), and `LSUPPVRS` (2014–15 only) remain source `*_code` plus readable yes/no/not-applicable labels. NCES X flags accompany each original numeric amount where supplied; notably collection flags `XLPCOLLC/XLECOLLC` and salary `XSALWAG` differ from a simple X + numeric field spelling. Source `LSWMSOM ` includes trailing whitespace in **2016–23**, normalized before selection.

### Human Resources

`HumanResources(...).run(dataset=...)` selects exactly **one of seven modern 2012–2024 files**. It preserves raw category codes and year-specific readable NCES dictionary labels in [hr_codes.csv](hr_codes.csv). `get_available_vars()` describes the union of family variables; `get_dataset_vars(dataset)` lists just the selected output columns. Source rows include **overlapping** all-staff totals, occupation details, faculty/tenure subtotals and all-rank salary totals; use `category_codes` to choose specific primary `EAPCAT`, `STAFFCAT`, `SISCAT`, `FACSTAT`, `SNHCAT`, `ARANK`, or noninstructional occupation codes `01`–`14` rather than summing entire files. These categories can also change definitions; the 2016 library/student-and-academic-affairs occupation labels are one visible break even **within** modern coverage.

| `dataset` | Source row grain and exposed values | Key distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staff` | `S*_OC`: institution × `STAFFCAT`, with `FTPT`/`OCCUPCAT`; total/men/women and race × sex headcounts | All/full-/part-time/graduate-assistant and occupational subtotal rows overlap. Not all institutions report detailed S*_OC in the earliest 2012 waves. |
| `employees` | `EAP*`: institution × `EAPCAT`; occupational/faculty status; FT/PT and medical versus nonmedical counts | Medical counts are source subcomponents; do not add EAP grand totals and detail categories. |
| `instructional_staff` | `S*_IS`: institution × `SISCAT`, `FACSTAT`, `ARANK`; full-time instructional counts by sex/race | All-rank and tenure/rank detail codes overlap; non-tenure contract categories evolve. |
| `faculty_ranks` | `S*_SIS`: institution × `FACSTAT`; separate rank counts across columns | **No sex or race** dimensions in this family. |
| `new_hires` | `S*_NH`: institution × `SNHCAT`, `OCCUPCAT`, `FACSTAT`; full-time new hires by sex/race | Reporting window changes from **July–October (4 months) through 2017** to **previous November–October (12 months) from 2018**. `hire_window_months` and `hire_period_start_year` expose it. |
| `instructional_salaries` | `SAL*_IS`: institution × `ARANK`; staff counts by sex/contract length and salary outlays/averages | Covers academic **year–year+1**, not fall payroll alone; all-rank code `7` overlaps rank codes `1`–`6`. |
| `noninstructional_salaries` | `SAL*_NIS`: institution × occupation `01`–`14`, made long from the 14 `SANIN##`/`SANIT##` pairs | Full-time counts and annual salary outlays **not disaggregated by sex**; occupation `01` is an overlapping total. |

`HR` staff headcounts are a **November 1 payroll snapshot**, not annual hours or unique hires. Salary outlays cover the academic year. `SAL*_IS` in **2012–15** supplies contract-month counts, total outlays and average months but **not** the source's comparable nine-month-equivalent salary average. **2016+** adds explicit 9/10/11/12-month salary averages and nine-month-equivalent averages by rank and men/women; the 2012–15 weighted-month method does **not** form a directly interchangeable trend. Months-worked/average-months fields found in early files disappear after 2017. Noninstructional outlay ÷ count is **not** a published individual salary or median. The APIs retain NCES `X*` reporting/imputation flags; absence of a category/measure is not a measured zero. [SAL2012_IS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/SAL2012_IS_Dict.zip), [SAL2016_IS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/SAL2016_IS_Dict.zip), [SAL2024_IS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/SAL2024_IS_Dict.zip), [S2024_OC](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/S2024_OC_Dict.zip).

These modern seven families have configured annual files for **2012–2024**. Older `SAL1984_A/B`, `S1987`, `SAL2004_A/B`, and other HR files exist but have **different occupation, contract, grain and benefit definitions** and are not quietly appended to this API. The [NCES 2012–13 SOC change](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/report-your-data/archived-changes/2012-13) specifically describes a loss of trend data; the [2018 SOC crosswalk](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/report-your-data/taxonomies-standard-occupational-classification-soc-codes) and year-specific dictionaries should guide any further category comparisons. No 2025–26 spring HR ZIP was confirmed at this research date. Labels in the installed API come from packaged `hr_labels.json`; the complete searchable original-year mapping with NCES dictionary URLs is [hr_codes.csv](hr_codes.csv).

### Completion, Completers, and Cip

**`Completion` counts awards by CIP, selected award level and major reporting category; it does not count unique graduates.** The `MAJORNUM` field is **absent in 1984–2000** C-A sources and available from **2001** (`1` first, `2` second). Default `major='both'` preserves old records with `major_type='unspecified'` and sums both modern categories within a CIP; `major='first'/'second'` rejects any requested pre-2001 years rather than silently labeling them. Compare the actual [C2000_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2000_A_Dict.zip) and [C2001_A](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2001_A_Dict.zip) dictionaries. Associate/bachelor/master raw AWLEVEL are 3/5/7; the doctor's award filter changes from **9 before 2010** to **17–19 from 2010**. The cleaner groups matching rows by institution and CIP, then calculates men/race shares from the summed counts. A field of study reported as a second major is **not an additional unique graduate**. Totals and aggregate CIP codes (e.g. grand total `99` and category-level CIP codes) can overlap detailed CIPs; **never sum all CIP rows** to estimate national awards.

**`Completers` counts people**, beginning **2012**, from modern C-B (one unduplicated institution total) or C-C (one unduplicated count per award level with age bands). Older 1990s C-`_B` means something else and is not part of this API. A person awarded degrees at multiple levels can occur in more than one C-C row; **summing C-C levels does not reproduce C-B**. C-C `AWLEVELC` of `03/05/07/09` (or 3/5/7/9 without zero padding) means associate/bachelor/master/doctor; C-A's 17–19 doctor codes should not be applied to C-C. For short certificates, code **1 before 2020** splits into **11/12 from 2020**. `source_award_code` preserves the original spelling; `award_level_code` normalizes leading zeroes. B has no age or field-of-study breakdown. [C2012_B](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2012_B_Dict.zip), [C2012_C](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2012_C_Dict.zip), [C2020_C](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/C2020_C_Dict.zip), [C2025_C](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/C2025_C_Dict.zip).

`Cip` parses each year's C-A dictionary (HTML or Excel `Frequencies`) to label that year's CIP codes. `Completion.run(get_cip_codes=True)` joins by **CIP and year**. CIP versions (including the 2020 taxonomy used in C2025_A) are not automatically converted into a single field-of-study ontology. Codes like `99` are totals. [C2025_A dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/C2025_A_Dict.zip).

### Graduation

`Graduation` selects bachelor's `SECTION=2`, `GRTYPE=8` adjusted cohort and `9` graduated; or associate's `SECTION=4`, `GRTYPE=29/30`, restricting `CHRTSTAT` to `12–13`. It pivots source counts by institution and calculates sex/race **graduation percentages at 150% of normal time** as graduates ÷ adjusted cohort × 100. A bachelor cohort is tracked about six years and an associate cohort about three; the row's `year` is **not** the year of admission or a simple count of all annual degrees. It is not the GR200 200% rate nor OM 4/6/8-year outcomes. Earlier and later race columns use different raw names (`GRRACE##` versus named `GRTOTL*` and race columns). [GR2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/GR2024_Dict.zip).

`Graduation()` defaults to the configured **2000–2024** series. Earlier GR files exist upstream but are not harmonized by this API; a 1980s GR filename is not evidence of a survey release.

### Graduation200

`GR200_08`–`GR200_24` are **not** a longer follow-up of the same `(id, year)` row as `Graduation(year)`. GR200 reports a **bachelor's** entering cohort (e.g. 2016 entrants at 2024 status) or a **less-than-four-year degree/certificate** cohort (2020 entrants at 2024 status); the latter is *not* an associate-only group. NCES first introduced GR200 in a **2008 supplemental** collection; `collection_phase` marks 2008 separately from 2009 onward. This source does not provide the sex/race disaggregation of GR. [GR200_08 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/GR200_08_Dict.zip), [GR200_24 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/GR200_24_Dict.zip).

For bachelor (`BA*`) or less-than-four-year (`L4*`) records the cleaner exposes the revised entering cohort, permitted exclusions, adjusted cohort and **NCES-published** cumulative completions/rates by **100%**, **150%**, and **200%** of normal time. `additional_exclusions_200` can make `adjusted_cohort_200` **different** from the 150% denominator. `completed_150_to_200` is incremental, whereas `completed_200` is cumulative. `still_enrolled` appears in the file **from 2011** and is missing earlier, not zero. Source flags for `BANC200A/L4NC200A` are unusually spelled `XBANC20A/XL4NC20A` and retained. The API does **not** clamp rates to 0–100 or recompute them; for example the revised 2014 source has a reported `BAGR100=196` at UNITID `482468` with status `R`, which merits scrutiny in analysis rather than silent alteration.

### Outcome Measures

`OutcomeMeasures` reports cohorts of degree/certificate-seeking undergraduate **entrants**, including first-time and non-first-time as well as full-/part-time students. `year` is the eight-year **status** year; the entry period starts `year-8` and ends `year-7`. `OM2024` follows **2016–17** entrants to August 2024. This is not the GR/GR200 first-time/full-time cohort, and the API has no individual wages or sex/race-by-outcome cuts. [OM2015 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/OM2015_Dict.zip), [OM2017 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/OM2017_Dict.zip), [OM2024 dictionary](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/complete-data-files/OM2024_Dict.zip).

**2015–16 initial scheme:** `OMCHRT=1/2/3/4` respectively identifies FT-first-time, PT-first-time, FT-non-first-time, PT-non-first-time. It has separate revised/excluded/adjusted **six- and eight-year** cohort fields; the 2015 `OMRCHT8` revised count is absent in 2016. Six- and eight-year published award counts/percentages are available, but **no four-year award count, Pell subgroup or award-type breakdown**. `OMFLAG=1` identifies a data inconsistency in the **2015 file only**; preserve it rather than discarding the row. `OMEXCL8` is described as an *additional exclusion* in the 2016 dictionary; do not read these initial fields as one modern shared denominator.

**2017+ expanded scheme:** `OMRCHRT/OMEXCLS/OMACHRT` form the revised/excluded/adjusted cohort. `OMCHRT=10/20/30/40` identifies the same four attendance/entry groups and `50` is the *overall total*. Codes ending `1` are Pell; ending `2` non-Pell (e.g. `11` FT-first-time Pell, `31` FT-non-first-time Pell); ending `0` is that group's total. The overall `50` and group totals **overlap**; each total also overlaps its Pell/non-Pell subsets. `run(cohort_type='all', pell_group='total')` keeps five totals per reporting institution where present; `pell_group='all'` includes overlapping subgroups. Pell or an overall-total request spanning 2015–16 raises rather than fabricating early observations. `source_cohort_code`, `schema_version` and `pell_group='not_collected'` in initial years make those distinctions explicit.

`awards_4/6/8` and `awards_*_pct` retain NCES's **reported** award counts and percentages at the reporting institution; 2017+ `certificate_*`, `associate_*`, `bachelor_*` identify the *highest award at each checkpoint*. `still_enrolled_here_8` and `subsequently_enrolled_elsewhere_8` describe known enrollment, while `enrollment_unknown_8` is explicitly unknown and `no_award_8` means no award **at the reporting institution**. They do not claim degrees earned elsewhere. Raw X flags for source counts and percentages remain `*_status`; unavailable early fields are missing rather than zero. Do not sum overall, subgroup or checkpoint rows as independent people.

## Missing data and source flags

- NCES source ZIPs often have `X...` companion columns with status codes such as **R** reported, **Z** implied zero, **A** not applicable and **B** blank. Other values identify particular corrections/imputation procedures; their exact definitions belong to the **year-specific dictionary**, not a single global code table. The newer `AcademicLibraries`, `Finance`, `DistanceEnrollment`, `Graduation200`, `OutcomeMeasures`, `Retention`, `Tuition`, `StudentAid`, `VeteransAid`, `HumanResources`, `TwelveMonthEnrollment`, `InstructionalActivity` and `Completers` APIs retain selected flags in `*_status` columns. Older `Admissions`, `Enrollment`, `Completion` and `Graduation` cleaners generally **do not** expose source flags.
- A column introduced in a later year is returned as missing (`NaN` for numeric, `<NA>` for a status), **not zero**, in the earlier years of APIs with fixed output schemas. A present source field can also be NA because it is not applicable, the institution did not report, or it was suppressed. The older aggregate cleaners may use pandas group sums that produce zero where all selected input values were missing; **do not assume their zeros always mean a measured zero**.
- The downloader accepts only configured subjects/endpoints, requests ZIPs over HTTPS with timeouts, bounds compressed/decompressed sizes, selects a CSV (or a CIP HTML/Excel dictionary) with preference for an `_rv` revision, and writes under a canonical subject-year filename rather than extracting archive-supplied paths. HTTP/ZIP failures raise and leave no partial cached year; completed years can be reused on retry. Recent files can be provisional and subsequently revised. The API does not attach a release-version column. The research [data release schedule](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/survey-components/data-release-schedule) distinguishes provisional and final releases. Record ZIP names and download dates in reproducible downstream analyses. `rm_disk=True` explicitly removes the chosen cache directory and should not be used for directories that contain other work.

## Maintaining this reference

Changes to `cfg.json` variable descriptions, `VARIABLE_RENAME`, or the source-field maps require `python codebook/build.py` and a review of the affected variable rows. **The generated CSV cannot discover semantic comparability from a column name.** Edit the curated [changes.csv](changes.csv), [codes.csv](codes.csv), and this guide when year/denominator/row-grain rules change; add dictionary links for the years checked. Check output-column coverage and generated snapshots using the workflow in [README.md](README.md). The package itself does not bundle `codebook/`.
